Last updated: 14 November 2026

Who we are

This policy explains how Fafnir Limited ("we", "us"), based in Trinidad and Tobago, handles personal information when you contact us or use our services, including through WhatsApp.

What we collect

  • Contact details you share with us, such as your name and phone number.
  • The content of messages you send us, for example on WhatsApp.
  • Information needed to provide a service you have asked for.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and provide the services you request.
  • To send you messages you have asked for, such as reminders or notifications.
  • To keep our services secure and working properly.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers we need to deliver our services (for example, the messaging platform you contact us through, such as WhatsApp, operated by Meta), or when required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above, then delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop messaging you, at any time by contacting us. On WhatsApp, you can also block our number.
